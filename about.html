About

I'm a product designer based in Jakarta, focused on editorial, cinematic digital experiences — the kind of interfaces that feel considered rather than assembled.

Over the years I've worked across fintech, e-commerce, and creative tooling, always chasing the same thing: clarity. I believe good design is mostly about removing, not adding, and that the best interactions are the ones you barely notice.

When I'm not pushing pixels I'm probably reading about typography, collecting film stills, or overthinking the border radius of something.
